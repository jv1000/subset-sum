## Readability, arXiv suitability and future work

EVIDENCE — The prose is mostly clear at the sentence level, the typography is conventional, and the central idea—improve a certified point while studying what limits the analysis—is worth communicating. The current obstacle is the reader's path through the paper. A reader encounters source-specific conditions, Corollary D, region certificates and unintroduced parameters before getting a sufficiently complete model. Repeated accounts of who checked what take space that would be better spent stating the usable lemmas and explaining the algorithm's dependencies.

EVIDENCE — I recommend a revision, not abandonment or waiting until the research program is complete. A conditional numerical theorem with a reusable rigorous certificate, a genuinely proved obstruction for a precisely stated budget, and clearly separated open conjectures can add value now. For arXiv, make the new arguments and frozen artifacts accessible and the claims honest; for a venue submission, also sharpen the novelty and relation to the imported proof.

EVIDENCE — The future-work agenda should end with four precise items: [S] at linear k (with general k=1,t>=3 still open), a proved joint-capacity bound or a certified better filter, alternatives to the full alias-enumeration phase, and the specifically unverified imported lemmas. Small-k special cases are progress toward [S], not a proof of the range needed by the ternary theorem. The sharper 0.4852 budget floor and capacities near 0.029 remain numerical evidence unless globally certified.
